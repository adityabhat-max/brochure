The Visionary Behind ISAAC Luxe
Dr. Geetika Mittal Gupta
Founder and Medical Director of ISAAC Luxe / Aesthetic Physician, and award-winning cosmetologist — Dr. Geetika Mittal Gupta is one of India's most sought-after skin experts.

With over 15 years of experience in aesthetics, Dr. Geetika has built a reputation for personalised care and delivering results with minimal risk. Her clientele includes celebrities, influencers, and discerning individuals who trust her expertise.

She is a pioneer in introducing globally advanced treatments to India, including REVIV IV Drip Therapy and fat-freezing technology, setting new benchmarks in luxury dermatology.

MBBS from Govt. Medical College, Patiala. Post-graduation in Practical Dermatology from Cardiff University, UK. Diploma in Aesthetic Medicine from the American Academy of Aesthetic Medicine.

🌍 Global Expertise

Attended numerous international workshops and conferences globally. Known for blending ancient and modern scientific techniques to deliver trusted, dermatologically tested treatments.

⭐ Celebrity Trusted

Go-to dermatologist for Bollywood celebrities, fashion icons, social media influencers, and high-profile personalities across India's entertainment and fashion industry.
