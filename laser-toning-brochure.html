How Does It Work?

Q-Switched Nd:YAG Laser

The Tribeam laser delivers short, high-energy pulses at specific wavelengths that are absorbed by melanin pigment. This shatters dark pigmentation deep in the dermis while leaving the epidermis completely intact â safe for all skin types including Indian skin tones.

Collagen Stimulation

The controlled laser energy also stimulates fibroblast activity in the dermis, boosting collagen and elastin production. This refines skin texture, minimizes pores, smooths fine lines, and delivers that coveted 'lit-from-within' glow over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does laser toning hurt?

Most clients describe it as a mild snapping sensation â like a rubber band lightly tapping the skin. No numbing cream is needed. The entire session takes just 20 minutes and is very tolerable.

How many sessions for melasma?

Melasma typically requires 8â10 sessions for significant improvement, followed by monthly maintenance. It's a chronic condition that can recur with sun exposure, so consistent SPF and maintenance sessions are key.

Is it safe for Indian skin?

Yes! The Q-Switched Nd:YAG laser is specifically safe for darker skin tones. Unlike some lasers that risk burns or hyperpigmentation on Indian skin, laser toning delivers precise energy without surface damage.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes! Laser toning pairs excellently with chemical peels, HydraFacial, AQUAGOLD, and topical prescription treatments. Dr. Geetika often creates combined protocols for comprehensive results.

Will pigmentation come back?

Some pigmentation (like melasma) can recur due to hormones, sun, or stress. Maintenance sessions and strict SPF use help prevent this. Sun damage pigmentation once cleared typically stays away with proper sun protection.

Can I do full body laser toning?

Absolutely! Laser toning works on face, neck, hands, arms, back, and any body area with pigmentation, dark spots, or uneven tone. ISAAC Luxe offers full-body laser toning sessions.
